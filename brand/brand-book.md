## 01 · Where we stand

### Audit of univlabs.in (October 2026)

| Finding | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| 3 spellings of the name | Univlabs in the logo, UnivLabs in copy, UNIVLABS in navigation |
| 3 competing taglines | "Care For All", "Better surgery. Everywhere.", "In service of humanity" |
| 13 names, four naming logics | Surgi-, Axis-, ".AI" and plain words, no family structure |
| 0 pages for Am-SafeX | The patented catheter and the hand instruments are missing from the site |

The fix is not a new logo. It is one set of rules that every team, agency and distributor follows.

### The story leads with what isn't built yet

The homepage headlines "autonomous robotics", "smarter AI", "the nervous system of surgery" and concept renders. All of these are in development or are metaphors. Meanwhile the deployed facts are strong: 250+ suites installed, 2,000+ devices supplied, 19 operating rooms on CogniAxis, 16 patents filed (4 granted).

**Rule:** lead with the deployed facts. The pipeline gets its own page, clearly labelled.

### Four audiences

| Audience | Priority | What they want |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Surgeons | Primary | Image quality, ICG performance, ergonomics, peer proof |
| Procurement and biomedical | Primary | Cost of ownership, uptime, service response, certificates |
| Distributors and partners | Secondary | Training, margins, support, export-ready documents |
| Investors | Secondary | Traction, IP, team pedigree, believable regulatory path |

Patients benefit from every decision but never buy. They appear in the brand as outcomes and evidence, not as stock smiles.

### The first frame is the surgeon's view

The instinct is to open with happy, healthy people. Every hospital, insurer and pharma brand already does that. UnivLabs opens with what only it can show: real footage from its own camera. People come second, and they are the right people: surgeons, OR teams and the engineers who build the systems.

## 02 · Positioning

### Positioning statement (internal)

> For hospitals and surgeons who want world-class minimally invasive surgery without import-level costs, UnivLabs designs and manufactures surgical vision systems, instruments and devices in India, backed by patented engineering, ISO 13485 manufacturing and a team from the world's leading medtech companies.

It deliberately leaves out AI, robotics and autonomy. Those belong in the pipeline story.

### Brand idea: Engineered for clarity.

- **Clarity of sight.** 4K, ICG and NIR imaging show surgeons what white light can't.
- **Clarity of cost.** Made in India, priced in the open, available per minute.
- **Clarity of claims.** We say what is deployed, and we label what is not.

Test for any new work: does this make something clearer?

### Lines

| Level | Line | Where |

### Message pillars

1. **Precision you can see.** 4K 4-chip ICG shows what white light can't. (Spectra, Lumina, SurgiStart, real footage)
2. **Built here, built to last.** Designed, manufactured and serviced in India. (ISO 13485, 16 patents, one-button service)
3. **Within reach.** World-class equipment more hospitals can afford. (Lower cost than imports, Pay Per Minute)
4. **Built with surgeons.** Ideas from the operating room become products. (SUFI, Am-SafeX, clinical co-founder)

CogniAxis, SurgiVault and SurgiBot are told through pillars 1 and 4. The future is never a fifth headline.

## 03 · Brand architecture

Masterbrand: **UnivLabs** leads every name on every surface.

| Family | Status | Members |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Surgical Vision | Live | Spectra, Lumina, SurgiStart, Components |
| Instruments & Devices | Live | Hand instruments, Am-SafeX |
| CogniAxis | Live | AxisEdge+ (hardware), SurgiVault (storage), Dr Mentor (teaching) |
| Programmes | Live | Pay Per Minute (leasing per minute of use), SUFI (surgeons pitch ideas, UnivLabs builds them), OBSR service |
| Research | In development | SurgiBot, biodegradable ureteral stent (with IIT Roorkee) |

### Naming

First mention: "UnivLabs Spectra 4K ICG suite", "UnivLabs Am-SafeX catheter", "UnivLabs CogniAxis", "SUFI, our surgeon co-innovation programme", "SurgiBot (research programme)". After that, the name alone. "CogniAxis.AI" survives only in its logo lockup and web address.

Rules for new names:
1. Hardware gets one word rooted in light or precision, like Spectra and Lumina.
2. No new "Surgi-" or "Axis-" prefixes. Existing names keep theirs.
3. No ".AI" or "+" in new names. Generations are numbered: Spectra 2.
4. Programmes get plain names. Acronyms are spelled out first.
5. Trademark search in India and export markets before any launch.

## 04 · Verbal identity

### Say the specific thing

| On the site today | Rewrite |
| --- | --- |
| "Better surgery. Everywhere." | 4K ICG imaging, designed and made in India. |
| "Connect your OR to the world." | Stream live surgery to authorised colleagues, securely. |
| "The nervous system of surgery." | Record, store and teach from every case. |
| "Towards intelligent surgical action." | SurgiBot is a research programme and is not available for clinical use. |

Fact first. One idea a sentence. Calm: sentence case, no exclamation marks.

### Claims policy

| Status | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Available | Claim within intended use. Specs, certificates, installs. Clinical benefits only as approved labelling states. |
| In development | Show, label, don't promise. No performance figures, no launch dates without sign-off. |
| Research | Talk about the question being studied. Visuals carry a "Concept render" label. |

Words we don't use: revolutionary, world's best, autonomous, AI-powered, guaranteed, zero risk, painless, cutting-edge.

Every external claim is signed off by the Director, Regulatory, before publishing.

### Style sheet

| Topic | Write | Never |

## 05 · Visual identity

### Logo

An evolution, not a redesign. The mark moves from black to Deep Navy, the wordmark stays UnivLabs Blue, and the tagline leaves the lockup. Final artwork must be rebuilt from the original vector files.

Six versions:
1. Full colour (navy mark, blue wordmark): default on light backgrounds.
2. Reversed (all white): on navy, blue and footage plates.
3. Navy mono: one-colour print, forms.
4. Single colour (black): laser etching, pad printing on instruments.
5. App icon (white mark on navy rounded square): software, favicon, device UI.
6. Avatar (white mark on blue circle): Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube.

Clear space: twice the height of one bar of the mark on every side.
Minimum size: full lockup 120 px / 25 mm wide; mark alone 24 px / 5 mm high (test etching on curved steel).

Don't: recolour the mark (green or otherwise); stretch, rotate or re-type the wordmark; add glows, shadows, gradients or 3D; place on busy footage without a navy plate; lock a tagline or product name to it; use the old black-mark artwork.

### Colour

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep Navy | #001A49 | Headlines, dark grounds |
| UnivLabs Blue | #0866F4 | Wordmark, links, buttons |
| ICG Green | #4BE779 | Signal only |
| Clinical White | #F4F6F9 | Page background |
| Paper | #FCFDFE | Cards |
| Steel | #5B6782 | Secondary text, labels |
| Line Grey | #D9DEE7 | Rules, borders |
| Blue 300 | #6FA3FF | Accent on navy |

Balance: 60 white · 25 navy · 10 blue · 5 green at most. Navy and blue are measured from the logo file; ICG Green is sampled from UnivLabs' own fluorescence footage. Proof CMYK/Pantone with the printer.

**The one accent rule: green means fluorescence.** Use it only where something is revealed: ICG footage, the NIR mode label, one key figure. Never on buttons, backgrounds or the logo, never as text on white (≈1.6:1). Its home is navy (≈10:1).

### Typography

| Typeface | Job |
| --- | --- |
| Plus Jakarta Sans | Headlines and numbers. Bold 700, tight tracking. |
| Inter | Body text, interfaces, documents. Regular 400, Semibold 600. |
| IBM Plex Mono | Labels, specs, mode names, sources. Uppercase, letter-spaced. |

Web scale: Display 64/1.05 · H1 44/1.1 · H2 28/1.25 · Body 18/1.6 · Label 13 mono. All free Google Fonts. Indian languages: matching Noto family. Office fallback: Arial.

### Graphic device: the bar system

Taken from the mark's rounded bars.
- **Mode selector:** the active imaging mode is the solid bar (website hero, product pages, Reels).
- **Roadmap:** solid bars are live, dashed bars are in development. No exceptions.
- **Section marker:** two short bars and one long bar open each section.

One thickness per layout, rounded ends, horizontal or vertical only, navy or blue. Green only for imaging.

### Imagery

Three kinds of picture, all real:
1. **The surgeon's view.** Footage from UnivLabs cameras, de-identified, consent on file. The hero of the brand.
2. **The product.** Studio shots on a cool grey sweep. True colour, soft light, no coloured gels, no floating renders.
3. **People at work.** Real surgeons, OR teams and UnivLabs engineers, documentary style, natural light, signed releases.

Footage rules: consent first (no names, dates or hospital marks burned in); label the mode in mono caps on every frame; in public, lead with ICG or NIR, never bleeding as a thumbnail. Sequence: white light → ICG overlay → NIR.

To commission: one product studio day, two documentary days (partner hospital OR, Gurugram facility).

### AI in our content

| Allowed | Only with a label ("Concept render") | Never |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Retouching dust and reflections | Future products such as SurgiBot | AI-generated surgeons, patients or staff |
| Extending a photo's background | System and data-flow illustrations | AI-generated tissue, organs or surgery |
| Subtitles and translations, checked by a person | Internal campaign concept boards | AI images shown as a real device |
| First drafts of copy, edited to this book | | AI-written claims published unreviewed |

The test: could a surgeon mistake it for a photograph of something real? Then it must be real.

## 06 · Applications

### Website homepage, top to bottom

1. Hero: real footage with the mode switch. "See what white light can't."
2. Proof bar with sources.
3. Three product families.
4. Specialty stories from surgeons.
5. Buy or Pay Per Minute.
6. Built with surgeons: SUFI.
7. Quality, regulatory, service.
8. Pipeline, clearly labelled.
9. Doors for hospitals, partners, investors.

Navigation: Products · Specialties · Connected OR · Work with us · Company. Every product page: real footage or photo, intended use, spec table in mono, certificates by market, brochure and IFU downloads, request a demo.

### Instagram

Purpose: a window into the OR and the lab. For surgeons and residents, engineers we want to hire, and partners abroad. Cadence: 3 feed posts a week (one a Reel), Stories for events and the lab floor.

Content mix: 30% the surgeon's view · 25% inside UnivLabs · 20% built with surgeons · 15% learn · 10% milestones (never more).

Bio: "Surgical systems, designed and made in India. / 4K ICG imaging · instruments · Am-SafeX / Gurugram · Bengaluru · Lille" + univlabs.in. Avatar: white mark on a blue circle.

Grid: every row mixes one piece of footage, one text card and one human or product moment. Grey dashed tiles are photos to commission; never fill them with stock or AI.

| Format | Size | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feed post | 1080 × 1350 (4:5) | One idea. Headline of 8 words at most. |
| Carousel | 1080 × 1350, 5–8 slides | Slide 1 hooks. Last slide sums up. |
| Reel | 1080 × 1920 (9:16) | Text clear of top 250 px and bottom 400 px. Captions on. |
| Story | 1080 × 1920 | Events, polls, links. Mark top-left. |
| Grid preview | 3:4 crop of 4:5 | Key content inside the centre 1080 × 1440. |

Post anatomy: mono label top-left naming the pillar, one headline in Plus Jakarta Sans, mark bottom-left. 64 px margins on 1080.

Captions: hook in the first line, then context, one proof point, one call to action. 3–5 specific hashtags (#laparoscopy #ICGfluorescence #urology #MadeInIndia). No emoji, or one arrow. Tag surgeons and hospitals only with written approval; no patient faces, names or case details. Alt text on every post, burned-in captions on Reels. Reply within a working day; no clinical advice; product complaints go to support and are logged.

### LinkedIn

Where the brand sells. Banner: brand line + descriptor + the three-mode strip (1128 × 191). Company page: 2–3 posts a week (installs with hospital approval, explainer carousels, hiring). Founder voices: Sunil Kumar Singh and Dr Anil Mandhani, weekly. Posts 1080 × 1350; PDF carousels for specs.

### Print and product

- Datasheets: A4, navy header, three-mode strip, specs in mono, certificates in the footer.
- Instruments: single-colour mark, laser-etched. Regulatory labelling and UDI always come first.
- Packaging: white carton, navy band with the family name, mono product label.
- Trade shows: a footage wall cycling the three modes, one brand line, a live demo tower.
- Stationery: card with mark on front, details on back. Email signature text-only, one link.
- Device screens: boot screen with the mark, CogniAxis in the palette, mode names in mono.
